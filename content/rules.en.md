# On October 30, show us your costume!

The DRC Halloween costume contest is back. Detailed rules are coming soon.

**How it works**

1. On contest day, scan the QR code on the poster.
2. Log in with your institutional email (McGill, Douglas or CIUSSS): you'll get a 6-digit code.
3. Enter your costume with 1 to 3 photos, and vote for your favourite. One vote per person.
